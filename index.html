Bernardo Modenesi(he/him)

Tenure-track Instructor in Biostatistics, Department of Population Health Sciences, Spencer Fox Eccles School of MedicineAdjunct Assistant Professor, Kahlert School of ComputingUniversity of Utah

I develop causal inference and machine learning methods for observational health studies. Two threads run through the work: using machine learning to handle high-dimensional confounding in causal questions, and bringing causal ideas into machine learning for responsible AI, such as finding where, and for whom, a clinical prediction model is unreliable.

I earned a Ph.D. in Econometrics and Quantitative Economics and a master's degree in Statistics at the University of Michigan. I was then a Data Science and AI Research Fellow at the Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society (MIDAS), working with Rocket Companies.
